Magic Snake Privacy Policy

English 简体中文

English

Magic Snake (“the App”) is a puzzle learning and creation application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Accounts and personal information

The App does not require an account. The developer does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or other personal information through a developer-operated server.

2. Data stored on your device

User-created Magic Snake shapes, app preferences, language settings, color settings, and locally cached Pro entitlement status are stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide App features and is not uploaded to a server operated by the developer.

3. File import and export

The App can import and export Magic Snake shape files. Files are accessed only when you explicitly choose an import or export action through the device’s file interface. The developer does not automatically upload these files.

4. Google Play purchases

The App may offer optional paid features through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. The App may receive purchase and entitlement information from Google Play so it can unlock or restore paid features. The developer does not receive or store your payment card or bank account details.

Google may process information independently when providing Google Play and payment services. That processing is governed by Google’s own privacy terms.

5. Advertising and analytics

The App does not display advertising and does not include a developer-operated advertising or usage analytics service.

6. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell personal information. The developer does not directly share user-created shapes, local preferences, or imported files with third parties. Information processed by Google Play in connection with app distribution or purchases is handled by Google under its own policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local App data remains on your device until you delete it through available App functions, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App, subject to the behavior of your device’s operating system and backups. Google Play purchase records are retained by Google according to Google’s policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App does not require children to submit personal information to the developer and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service. Parents or guardians with privacy questions may use the developer contact information shown on the Magic Snake listing in Google Play.

9. Security

The App is designed to keep user-created content and preferences on the device. No method of electronic storage or third-party platform processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but the developer limits data handling to what is needed for the App’s features.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when App features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information provided on the Magic Snake Google Play listing.

简体中文

Magic Snake（“本应用”）是一款用于学习和创作魔尺造型的应用。本隐私政策说明您使用本应用时相关信息的处理方式。

1. 账号与个人信息

本应用不要求注册或登录账号。开发者不会通过自建服务器直接收集您的姓名、电子邮箱、电话号码、精确位置、通讯录、照片、广告标识符或其他个人信息。

2. 设备本地数据

用户创作的魔尺造型、应用偏好、语言设置、配色设置以及本地缓存的 Pro 权益状态保存在您的设备本地，用于提供应用功能，不会上传至开发者运营的服务器。

3. 文件导入与导出

本应用支持导入和导出魔尺造型文件。只有在您主动选择导入或导出操作时，本应用才会通过系统文件界面访问您选择的文件。开发者不会自动上传这些文件。

4. Google Play 购买

本应用可能通过 Google Play 提供可选的付费功能。付款由 Google Play 处理。本应用可能从 Google Play 获取购买及权益状态信息，以解锁或恢复付费功能。开发者不会获取或保存您的银行卡、信用卡或银行账户信息。

Google 在提供 Google Play 和付款服务时可能独立处理相关信息，该处理行为受 Google 自身隐私条款约束。

5. 广告与分析

本应用不展示广告，也不包含由开发者运营的广告服务或用户行为分析服务。

6. 数据共享与出售

开发者不会出售个人信息，也不会主动向第三方分享用户创作的造型、本地偏好或导入的文件。Google Play 在应用分发或购买过程中处理的信息由 Google 按其自身政策处理。

7. 数据保留与删除

本地应用数据会保留在您的设备上，直到您通过应用提供的功能删除相关内容、清除应用存储或卸载应用；具体行为也可能受设备操作系统及备份机制影响。Google Play 的购买记录由 Google 按其政策保留。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不要求儿童向开发者提交个人信息，也不会通过开发者运营的服务故意收集儿童个人信息。家长或监护人如有隐私问题，可使用 Magic Snake 在 Google Play 商品详情中显示的开发者联系信息与开发者联系。

9. 安全

本应用的设计原则是将用户创作内容和偏好保留在设备本地。任何电子存储方式或第三方平台处理都无法保证绝对安全，但开发者会将数据处理限制在实现应用功能所必需的范围内。

10. 本政策的更新

当应用功能、第三方服务或法律要求发生变化时，本隐私政策可能会更新。本页顶部的“最后更新”日期用于标识最新版本。

11. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请使用 Magic Snake 的 Google Play 商品详情 中提供的开发者联系信息。
